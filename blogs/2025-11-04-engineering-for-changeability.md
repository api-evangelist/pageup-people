Introduction Quality isn’t a checkbox or a mood — it’s achieved as a culmination of countless small decisions, habits, and architectural choices that make code easier to change over time. As software engineers, we often chase quality like it’s a destination, but it’s more like terrain we shape as we go. And just as “one does not simply walk into Mordor”, one does not simply throw on their “quality coding pants” and decide to do quality today.
