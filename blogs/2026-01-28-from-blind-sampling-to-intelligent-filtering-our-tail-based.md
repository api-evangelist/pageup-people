What we learned building enterprise-grade Refinery infrastructure Observability is critical for modern distributed systems, but it comes with a challenge: telemetry data can grow exponentially, leading to skyrocketing costs. At PageUp, we faced this exact problem with our Honeycomb telemetry. While we needed comprehensive visibility into our systems — especially errors and performance issues — sending 100% of our traces to Honeycomb was becoming unsustainable.
