It was back in 2019 just before the whole COVID pandemic began, when PageUp ventured into the land of observability. It started just as: We completed a multi-account architecture in PageUp We started realising we were getting more microservices in production Our continuous deployment pipelines were firing and getting many deployments out to production We have also started to adopt containers and were favouring managed container hosting using ECS Fargate. We have heard stories about organisations using technologies like Splunk, Sumo Logic and Datadog.
