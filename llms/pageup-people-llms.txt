# PageUp People

> PageUp is an enterprise talent management and talent acquisition software provider (Melbourne, Australia) serving large organizations across government, healthcare, higher education, and retail. The platform spans recruitment marketing, applicant tracking (ATS), onboarding, performance, learning, succession, and analytics, and exposes an OAuth 2.0-secured REST API suite for clients and integration partners.

## APIs

- [PageUp Talent Management API](https://developers.pageuppeople.com/): OAuth 2.0 client-credentials REST API suite. Endpoint groups: Core HR (Employee, Position, Business Unit, Cost Centre, Agreement, Agreement Classification, Agreement Type, Pay Scale, Role, Site, Team, Generic List), Recruitment (Applicant, Applications, Document, Hire), Partner (Learning Activities, SCORM Content, Assessment, Background Checking, Work Compliance), Performance (Performance Review Reports), Platform (Exports). Per-tenant API domains are provisioned by PageUp.
